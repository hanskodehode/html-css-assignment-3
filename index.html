The Sun

  • The Sun is a star at the center of our solar system.
  • It’s about 4.6 billion years old.
  • Its diameter is roughly 1.39 million kilometers (865,000 miles).
  • More than a million Earths could fit inside it by volume.
  • The Sun is made mostly of hydrogen and helium.
  • Its core reaches about 15 million °C (27 million °F).
  • Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun. Its surface is covered with craters, and because it has almost no atmosphere to trap heat, temperatures swing from scorching hot during the day to freezing cold at night. A year on Mercury lasts just 88 Earth days.
